## Editorial and privacy limits

- First-person reflections are suggested wording for the author's review;
  they are not quotations extracted from visa documents.
- Attendance and the IJCNN experiences listed by the author are factual
  starting points. A planned activity is not recast as a confirmed memory.
- There are no invented dialogues, named encounters, missed connections,
  medical events, dramatic incidents, or sensory details.
- Passport data, visa numbers, home addresses, phone numbers, travel-document
  access codes, booking identifiers, hotel details, and financial records
  are not embedded or linked from the public-facing articles.
- Visa, itinerary, and original research files are read only.
- These are retrospective essays, not current travel advice or an assertion
  that historic transport and attraction arrangements remain available.
- NeurIPS 2026 is still in the future on the composition date and is not
  included as a completed journey.
