My July 2026 trip to Seoul had a fixed point: COEX, where the International Conference on Machine Learning took place from July 6 to 11. Around it, I arranged eight days and seven nights, from July 5 to 12.

The itinerary reached beyond Seoul to Suwon and Incheon. The conference supplied the reason to travel; the plan gave the rest of the week a shape.

## A question about search

The work was [The Geometric Reasoner](/publications#publication-geometric-reasoner), a method for organizing search during long-context reasoning.

Earlier projects had focused on understanding a question, generating an answer and checking it. This one moved my attention toward the possible paths through a reasoning problem. Extra computation gives a model more chances to search, but those chances can be spent repeating similar paths.

The method uses the geometry of the model's internal representations to guide exploration. A lightweight look-ahead estimate helps compare candidate directions, while limits on the working memory keep the search manageable.

I was interested in a question that also runs through my work on inference efficiency: how to make additional computation useful. A larger budget is only part of the answer. The way it is spent matters too.

## Around Seoul

The city plan grouped Gyeongbokgung Palace with Seochon, Insadong and Ikseon-dong. Another part stayed closer to the conference area, with Bongeunsa, Seonjeongneung and Samseong-dong.

Changdeokgung Palace and its garden, N Seoul Tower, the National Museum of Korea and Cheonggyecheon also had places in the schedule. The list mixed palaces and museums with neighborhoods and public spaces.

I like that mixture. A museum can give a day a clear destination; a neighborhood leaves more decisions to the walk itself. Both deserve time, even when the trip began with a conference invitation.

## Suwon and the way home

I set aside a day for Suwon. The planned route linked Paldalmun, Hwaseong Haenggung, Haenggung-dong and Janganmun, giving the week a stop outside Seoul.

The final day brought Incheon into the itinerary, with Yeongjong Island and Eurwangni Beach before the airport. The journey home had a place in the plan rather than taking up an unmarked space at the end.

Two years had passed since [my first flight to Japan](/blog/ijcnn-2024-yokohama-first-journey). I was taking a different paper to a different city, with a different set of questions to explain. The trips make those changes easier to place in time.

Later that summer came [IJCAI in Bremen and a longer European route](/blog/ijcai-2026-bremen-europe).
