Some thoughts begin with a research question. Others come from a book, a conversation, a walk, or a quiet moment in an ordinary day. This blog has room for all of them.

## What belongs here

Papers and projects have their own places on this website. Here, a note can be a personal essay, an unfinished idea, or something small that feels worth remembering.

Some things worth writing down:

- A question that follows me home from work.
- A passage in a book that changes how I see something.
- A small observation from daily life.
- A place, a conversation, or a moment I want to return to.

## Writing without a fixed theme

Not every entry needs to lead to a result or make an argument. Some can be practical; others can simply describe an experience or leave a question open.

Research will be part of this journal, alongside reading, daily life, and whatever else catches my attention.

## Keeping things small

There is no need for a fixed publishing schedule. A short note is enough, and an older entry can always be revisited when a thought changes.
